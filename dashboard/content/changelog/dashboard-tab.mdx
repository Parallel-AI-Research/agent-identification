---
title: A Dashboard tab
description: Get back to your products from anywhere in the dashboard.
date: 2026-11-02
---

Inside the dashboard, a Dashboard tab now sits on the right of the navbar, beside your account menu. It takes you back to your list of products from any product page.

On phones and tablets, Dashboard is in the menu, above your email and Sign out.

## Fixed

- **Tablets:** the home page and changelog menu had no way into the dashboard at tablet widths. It now shows Dashboard beside the theme toggle.
