The public site, docs and dashboard now share one navbar.

## What's new

- **Navbar:** Docs and Changelog sit on the left on every page, including the docs.
- **Dashboard:** a button at the far right takes you there. Once you're inside, it becomes an account menu with your email and Sign out.
- **Dark mode:** switch between light and dark from the navbar, and your choice is remembered.

## What's gone

- **The old demo page:** the "Try the demo" button and the page it opened have been removed. The docs and quickstart replace them.
