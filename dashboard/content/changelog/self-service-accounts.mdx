You can now create an account and set up a product yourself.

## What's new

- **Accounts:** sign up, confirm your email, and reset your password yourself.
- **Products:** add a product with its exact origins and get a snippet with your own site ID.
- **Domain verification:** add a DNS record to show you control a domain. Your data arrives either way.
- **Secret rotation:** rotate a server secret with 24 hours of overlap, or revoke the old one straight away.

## Under the hood

Each product now has environments, each with its own site ID, secret and origins. New products start with production. Adding others from the dashboard is coming soon.

Existing installs keep their site IDs and secrets, so they don't need any changes.
