## What's new

- **Delete a product:** at the bottom of its page. It removes every environment, its site IDs and secrets, and everything they recorded. You confirm by typing the product's name, and it can't be undone.
- **Troubleshooting and uninstalling:** a [guide](/docs/troubleshooting) to what each status means, why the widget or events might not appear, and how to remove an install.
