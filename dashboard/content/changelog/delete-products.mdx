## What's new

- **Delete a product:** at the bottom of its page, confirmed by typing its name. Every environment's install stops within 30 seconds.
- **Restore it for 30 days:** deleted products are listed under **Recently deleted** on **Your products**. Restoring brings back the same site IDs, secrets and origins, so installs start again, with whatever its sites recorded that hasn't expired.
- **Delete for good:** after 30 days, or straight away with **Delete now**, the product, its secrets and everything it recorded are removed. That can't be undone.
- **Troubleshooting and uninstalling:** a [guide](/docs/troubleshooting) to what each status means, why the widget or events might not appear, and how to remove an install.
