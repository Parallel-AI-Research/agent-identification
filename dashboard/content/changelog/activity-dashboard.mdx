Each product now has an **Activity** page built on what your install actually collected.

## What's new

- **Overview:** sessions, page views, signed requests and confirmed actions, counted separately for the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. A retry is never counted twice.
- **One label per session:** Verified signer, Declared AI, Suspected automation, Declared person or Unknown, with no score. Unknown never means a person.
- **Every signal kept:** each session's timeline shows every event and who vouches for it. When signals disagree, the session says so instead of picking one.
- **Environments:** switch between production and your other environments. The choice stays in the link, and each environment's numbers stay separate.
- **Add an environment:** add beta, staging or development from the product page, then copy its own snippet and secret.

See [Activity](/docs/activity) for how labels work.
