---
title: Troubleshooting and uninstalling
description: What to check when something doesn't show up, and how to remove an install.
---

## Nothing shows up

Start with **Status** on the product page. It only changes on what the collector actually received.

- **Waiting for installation:** no page on one of this environment's origins has loaded the widget. Check that the snippet is on the page, that the page's origin is listed exactly (`https://www.example.com` and `https://example.com` are different), and that you're looking at the right environment.
- **A page tried to load the widget, but this environment doesn't allow that origin:** add the origin under **Website origins** if the site is yours.
- **A server request used a secret this environment doesn't accept:** check `AGENT_ID_SITE_SECRET` against the current secret. After a rotation, the old one works for 24 hours.
- **The widget sent a binding context the collector couldn't verify:** your server signed it with an old secret, or it expired. Contexts last up to 15 minutes.

## The widget doesn't appear

- **Content Security Policy:** allow the collector in `script-src`, `style-src` and `connect-src`.
- **The browser console** shows a line starting `Visitor identification unavailable` with the reason, and the page receives an `agent-identification:error` event.

## Page views, actions or signed agents are missing

- **Page views** need SDK 0.4.0 or later, and stop when `data-page-views="off"` is set.
- **Actions** only join a session when `sessionBinding` matches the binding your server put in the widget's binding context. Without one, they're counted as actions without a session.
- **Signed agents** need your server to report signed requests. An **Unknown signer** result means the agent isn't on the allowlist yet; it's still recorded.
- Every helper resolves to `failed` with a reason instead of throwing. Pass `onError` to log it.

## Uninstalling

1. Remove the snippet or `init()` call from your pages.
2. Remove `reportAction()`, `reportSignedRequest()` and `reportRequestSignals()` from your server, and delete `AGENT_ID_SITE_SECRET`.
3. To remove the data too, delete the product at the bottom of its page. That removes its environments, site IDs and secrets, and everything they recorded, and installs stop working within 30 seconds.

Without deleting, sessions are removed after seven days and activity after 30.
